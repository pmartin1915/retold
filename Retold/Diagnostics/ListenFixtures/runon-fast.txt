So we packed up the car that night and drove straight through to Memphis because my brother had a job interview in the morning and he was so nervous he could not sleep so he kept talking the whole way about the trucks and the radio stations and the kind of house he wanted to buy someday with a big porch and a swing and a garden for tomatoes and peppers and when we finally got there the sun was coming up over the river and we ate breakfast at a diner where the waitress called us honey and refilled our coffee about six times and then he went in to the interview in a borrowed tie and came out an hour later grinning like he had won the lottery because they hired him on the spot and he said we should celebrate so we drove down Beale Street and listened to an old man play the blues on a guitar with only five strings and my brother gave him every dollar in his wallet and we laughed about that for years because we had to borrow gas money from our mother to get back home.
