The first thing I remember is the smell of the barn. It was cold that morning, and my father had already been out there for an hour. I think I was five or six. He let me hold the bucket while he milked the cow, and I spilled half of it on my boots. He did not get angry. He just laughed and said that the cats would be happy. I have thought about that laugh a lot since he passed. Some days it is the only thing I can hear clearly.
